How to play

Every puzzle starts and ends on a medical term, and its meaning is shown under the keyboard. Rungs in between can be any real word, but medical words you use are marked with a cross and added to the list. Hints take the route with the most medical words.

Start at the top word. Each new rung must be a real four-letter word that differs from the rung above by exactly one letter, in the same position. Reach the target in as few rungs as you can.

Par is the shortest possible ladder. Type with your keyboard or tap the keys. A hint shows the next word on a shortest route from where you are. Target letters turn green once they are in place.

With a partner, you take turns on the same five puzzles. Your score for a round is rungs plus hints, and the lower score wins it. You only see your partner's ladder once you have both played. Partner games need the internet; solo play works offline.

Lewis Carroll invented this puzzle in 1877 and called it Doublets. His first example turned HEAD into TAIL: head, heal, teal, tell, tall, tail.
